## 0. TL;DR

- **What the app is today:** Phase 1 shell only — login/TOTP, members+invites, account page, plain Tailwind, no component kit, no i18n, no domain tables beyond auth/tenancy/documents. Your "MVP" read is correct.
- **What it becomes:** *"the agency's operating system with a client window"* — Azure DevOps' **data model** (generic work item, category-typed states, single rank, Epic→Task→Subtask, field history) under Planner/Linear's **surface** (title-only create, inline everything, one board per project, ⌘K, a personal Home), plus the three things none of those ship: **first-class time & cost** (per-task timer → rate snapshot → budget → rollups per member/team/project/client → invoice line), a **client portal that is a projection of the same rows** under the existing RESTRICTIVE RLS visibility model (selective sharing, progress reports with a timeline, deliverables/assets), and a **per-client credential vault + asset registry with expirations** next to the project.
- **Explicitly NOT an ADO clone.** ADO's reviewers call it "not intuitive / overkill for small teams / not for business people" — your client persona. We copy its schema, not its configuration surface (no process templates, area/iteration paths, WIQL, delivery plans, dashboard builders, sprints-by-default).
- **Execution order (Naxdor on boards by ~week 10, timer+cost by ~week 14):** `1b → 2 → 2W → 2T → 3 → 3V → 5 → 4 → 6 → 7 → 8`. Three new phases (2W Work, 2T Time, 3V Vault); Phase 5's `Issue` is absorbed into `WorkItem`; existing phase numbers stay stable so all 8 docs keep agreeing.
- **Three founder decisions get reversed/recorded** (dated, in OPEN_QUESTIONS.md): #11 time tracking leaves the skip list (with a hard *never-list* — no idle detection/screenshots/URL capture/presence/leaderboards, ever); #12 the vault is a product module with server-side envelope encryption (the continuity box stays pointer-only); #13 who must have MFA to reveal credentials.
- **One vocabulary trap to settle first:** your "*cost per hour for that project*" almost certainly means the **hourly rate you charge the client** (Fortleva: *bill rate*, project-scoped) × hours = what the work costs the client. Fortleva also supports an optional **internal cost rate** (salary-derived, encrypted, CEO/finance-only) for margin. Both are in the plan; the UI labels them "Rate / Value" vs "Internal cost / Margin". Confirm at CP0.
- **Step 0 of implementation is docs, not code:** the 8 spec docs are the source of truth and currently contradict this direction (skip-list, "pointers not secrets", Issue entity, 63-code catalog test). They get dated amendments first, then the build proceeds phase by phase with brainstorm checkpoints CP0–CP6.

## 2. What the research found (condensed)

**Convergent skeleton of the best tools (ADO, Linear, Plane, Planner-premium, Huly):** one generic work-item record (type, state, single assignee, priority, labels, links, description, comments, attachments, field-level history); states carry a fixed *category* (backlog/todo/started/done/cancelled) so custom names never break rollups; a parent pointer for hierarchy with progress computed from categories; a board that is a *view* over states (drag = state change + rank change); an ordered backlog where position *is* priority (fractional rank); one universal saved-view abstraction (filters + group + order + layout) rendering My Work / backlog / board / portal list; a personal home ("My Work") that is why people open the app daily.

**Where they all stop — and agency suites fill in identically (Teamwork, Productive, Kimai, OpenProject, Harvest, Toggl):** one running timer per person stored as an open row (`stoppedAt NULL`, partial unique index); rate hierarchy *most-specific-wins*, **snapshotted onto each entry** at write; cost rate kept separate and permission-gated; budgets in hours-or-money with once-per-threshold alerts; entries locked once invoiced; raw seconds stored, rounding only at report/invoice.

**Client sharing converged on Basecamp:** private by default, per-item flag with a loud badge, children inherit, comments have their own switch, "view as client" preview. **Progress reporting converged on Asana/Linear:** an immutable status post with a *human-chosen* health signal and *machine-filled* metrics, on a cadence enforced by reminders; the client sees one page ("how is it going, what's next, what do you need from me, what did I get"), never a board they must learn.

**Vault/assets converged on Hudu/IT Glue:** credentials live *next to* the client/project/asset (not a detached password manager), typed items, masked by default, reveal is an explicit audited act with step-up, expiring/view-once share links, an asset registry (domain/hosting/DNS/SSL/licences) with a unified expirations feed. E2EE was tried by Infisical/IT Glue and dropped/became a support burden — server-side envelope encryption with AAD + audit is the honest, exportable choice for 3–15 person agencies.

**What "user-friendly" concretely means (from complaint corpora):** perceived latency < ~50 ms (Jira = most-hated for slowness/200-field forms); title-only creation with no modal; every property inline-editable; drag anywhere with instant optimistic reflow; ⌘K + single-key shortcuts; opinionated defaults with escape hatches (5 priorities, ~5 states, no process picker); one place per concept (Project → Board | Backlog | Time | Updates | Files | Portal); explicit filter chips (Planner's hidden filters are a top complaint); digest-not-firehose notifications; explicit archive instead of ADO's silent 183-day disappearance; nothing a non-technical client must learn.

**Legal (Swedish/EU) — decisive for defaults:** a self-started/stopped timer with manager-visible totals is ordinary *tidsredovisning* (IMY: permitted, contract-necessary; consent is NOT a valid basis in employment). It becomes *övervakning* (DPIA-mandatory, MBL 11 § "viktigare förändring" for kollektivavtal-bound employers, NY/CT/DE notice statutes) the moment the system captures activity the employee did not volunteer. Cost rates are salary-grade personal data. Invoiced time entries are räkenskapsinformation (Bokföringslagen 7 kap 2 § — 7 years). ⇒ never-list, deny-default aggregate views, encrypted cost rates never fanned onto entry rows, staff notice + acknowledgment before first timer, retention split.

**Stack (verified against npm/Next docs 2026-08-16; re-verify versions when pinning):** `@atlaskit/pragmatic-drag-and-drop` 3.x (desktop; touch drag unreliable — GH #93 — so mobile gets "Move to…"), `fractional-indexing` 4.x + `text COLLATE "C"`, React 19 `useOptimistic` + Server Actions (no TanStack Query, no sync engine), Tiptap 3, TanStack Table v9 + Virtual, `cmdk`, `react-hotkeys-hook`, `nuqs`, Recharts 3, `date-fns` 4 + `@date-fns/tz`, `@react-pdf/renderer` (fra1 → R2), Postgres FTS with per-row `regconfig` (no GIN under FORCE RLS — non-leakproof quals never become index quals), Postgres outbox + `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` + Vercel Cron (Pro) + `after()` kicks, `@aws-sdk/client-sesv2`, shadcn/ui-style copy-in components on Radix + Tailwind 4 (ARC-15).

### 3.1 Vocabulary, hierarchy, states, assignment
| Pin | Decision |
|---|---|
| Entity | One `WorkItem` table. UI word = **"Task"**; levels are **Epic → Task → Subtask**. `depth` 0-indexed: EPIC=0 only; TASK 0 or 1; SUBTASK 1 or 2; `CHECK depth <= 2`, parent type strictly higher, same project, acyclic (trigger). Epic optional, hidden until used. No "Feature" level. |
| Kind | `kind {TASK, BUG, REQUEST}` on the same row. `REQUEST` = portal/email-submitted, starts in a `TRIAGE`-category state. **This absorbs the planned `Issue`**; `IssueComment` becomes the polymorphic `Comment`. |
| Human key | `Project.key` (≤ 8 chars, unique per tenant, e.g. `ACME`) + `WorkItem.number` from `TenantCounter` key `work_item:<projectId>` via one `counters.next(tx, key)` helper (`INSERT … ON CONFLICT DO UPDATE … RETURNING`). Display `ACME-12`. Per-project numbering (CP1 can revisit). |
| States | `WorkflowState` per project: tenant-named states inside fixed `StateCategory {BACKLOG, TODO, IN_PROGRESS, DONE, CANCELLED, TRIAGE}`; category immutable per state (trigger). Tenant-level `WorkflowPreset` copied at project creation; default preset = Backlog / To do / In progress / Done / Cancelled + a **hidden Triage** state auto-created per project (shown only when it has items). Portal only ever sees *categories* (i18n labels Planned / In progress / Done). |
| Denormalisation | `WorkItem.stateCategory` denormalised (kept in sync by the state service + trigger) — `WorkflowState` is class A and can never be joined from a contact principal. Portal reads `stateCategory` only. |
| Assignment | Single `assigneeMemberId` **xor** `assigneeContactId` (column lands in 2W; portal UI in 3; contact assignee ⇒ CLIENT_VISIBLE by CHECK) + `WorkItemCollaborator` join. No multi-assignee. |
| Ordering | One `rank text COLLATE "C"` per item per project (fractional-indexing), unique `(tenantId, projectId, rank)`, computed server-side with neighbour `SELECT … FOR UPDATE`, retry with jitter on collision, rebalance when a key > 50 chars. Backlog = all open by rank; board column = that state's items by rank (ADO "maintain backlog order"). No `WorkItemPlacement`/sprint order tables in v1. |
| Checklist | Tiptap taskList nodes inside the description; `checklistTotal/Done` denormalised on save; "convert to subtask" action. No `ChecklistItem` table. |

| Priority | `{NONE, LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH, URGENT}`. |

| Home | Route **`/home`** ("My Work"). `/dashboard` becomes the workspace picker only when > 1 membership. |

## 5. UX rules (write once into `docs/UI.md`; apply in every phase)
1. **Latency is a feature** — every mutation optimistic (`useOptimistic` + Server Action + `refresh()`), rollback toast; side-peek navigation; no modal for create.
2. **Title-only creation** — `C` anywhere / `+` in any list or column; Enter creates next; `⌘⇧Enter` create-another-same-props; `⌘Enter` create-and-open.
3. **Inline edit everything** via `<PropertyPicker>` popovers with single keys `S A L P E D V M T X`; no Save buttons anywhere.
4. **Drag with a keyboard twin** — drop indicator + auto-scroll (desktop); "Move to…" in menu/palette/mobile sheet; rank never shown.
5. **One board per project, columns are states, position is priority**; portal only sees categories.
6. **One universal `<WorkItemView>`** for Home, backlog, board, portal list — URL-addressable.
7. **⌘K first, single keys second, shortcuts shown everywhere** (tooltips, palette rows, `?` overlay).
8. **Home is the home page** — assigned/overdue/next-7 + timer + inbox + triage; timer one click from Home.
9. **The timer is a personal tool** — pill on every page, one tap to stop, auto-stop-previous with undo, forgotten-timer nudge, `1h 30m` input, raw seconds preserved.
10. **Visibility is loud, explicit, inherited, previewable** — two-token badge, composer asks, children follow, two-mode comment composer, "View as client", mention warning.
11. **Progress = human health + machine numbers**, immutable once published; client sees one screen and an action-items inbox.
12. **Digest, not firehose**; **explicit over silent** (archive/restore, visible chips, locked entries explain why, rate changes ask "from today / date / all unbilled").
13. **Opinionated defaults with escape hatches**: 5 priorities, ~5 states, hours, single assignee, depth 3; no process picker beyond "pick a project template".
14. **Cost is sensitive**: employees see hours, managers bill rates, finance cost; nothing per-member reaches the portal; no presence/activity/leaderboard UI anywhere.
15. **Empty states have a verb; onboarding is skippable**; density compact by default; every entity has an `<EntityChip>`; destructive actions confirm inline; **mobile** = bottom tabs Home / Board / Timer / Inbox / More, single-column list with state chips, no touch drag.

## 10. Risks & pushback (stated once, in the founder's own frame)
1. **"Like Azure DevOps … and much more" is the wrong target** — copy its data model, ship Planner/Linear's surface; every "much more" item is a later entitlement-gated module. If the plan bloats, the solo build fails on maintenance, not features.
2. **Time tracking is a legal object in Sweden.** Self-reported timer = fine; anything captured without the employee's act = övervakning. Naxdor needs the staff notice + acknowledgment (and an MBL 13 § check if any union member) before its own staff use timers. Cost rates are salary-grade data — hence encrypted, never on entry rows.
3. **The vault is a liability you are choosing** (Copilot/Moxie/SuiteDash ship none). Server-side crypto means the operator can technically decrypt — say so in the DPA/ROPA. Mitigations are procedural and tested before UI. E2EE would remove that risk but breaks search/share/TOTP/portal submission and strands 3-person agencies (Infisical dropped it; IT Glue's Vault is a support burden).
4. **The portal is where the worst bug lives** — the new surfaces (search index, notification fan-out, digest bodies, update snapshots, share links, mixed-visibility threads) multiply leak vectors; every one ships with its tripwire test in the same commit.
5. **RLS performance edges** are real (GIN/trgm dead under FORCE RLS; recursive CTEs re-evaluate policies) — hence narrow search_index, flat rollups on denormalised columns, bounded recursion.
6. **Docs are stale and contradict this direction** — Step 0 fixes that with dated entries, not silent overwrites.
7. **Date-fragile stack claims** get a "verified on" note in their ARC line and are re-checked when pinned.
8. **Two UX traps avoided on purpose:** Planner's checklist-as-subtask abuse + per-plan labels (→ checklist in description + convert; tenant-wide labels); ADO's hidden Board Column/Lane fields + per-team settings (→ one board per project, columns = states, single rank).
